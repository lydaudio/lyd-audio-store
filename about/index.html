ABOUT

lyd audio is a small line of audio effects and instruments for Windows. Every one of them started with a sound a record needed. Either no plugin made it, or the one that did cost too much and hid it behind a hundred controls. Goose is free, and it's the full synth, not a demo. Anything paid is yours to keep once you buy it, with no license key, no account and nothing to renew.

It's one person building these, so updates are slow. Word of mouth is what changes that. More people hearing about it means more time for new plugins, and more of them built around what people actually ask for.
